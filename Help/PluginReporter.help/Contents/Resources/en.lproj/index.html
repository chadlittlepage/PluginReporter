Your Complete Guide to Audio Plugin Management

Welcome to Plugin Reporter

Plugin Reporter is a powerful macOS application that helps you catalog, organize, and manage your audio plugins across all major formats (AU, VST, VST3, AAX, CLAP, LV2). Whether you're a music producer, sound designer, or audio engineer, Plugin Reporter provides the tools you need to keep track of your plugin library.
